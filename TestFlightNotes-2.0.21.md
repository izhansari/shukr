## What to Test (paste this)

What's new since the last build. Thank you for testing!

APPLE WATCH - PINCH TEST
- This build records every pinch on the watch so we can see why some pinches don't count.
  Try: On the watch, start a zikr and pinch slowly 10 times, then Finish.
  Try: Start again and pinch fast 10 times, then Finish.
  Try: Start again and turn the Crown 5 clicks, then Finish.
  Then, with the watch near the phone: on the phone, Settings > Send watch pinch log, and send it to Izhan.
- The watch's Settings rows fit on the smaller 41 mm watch.

POST-SALAH TASBIH
- Opening and closing the Tasbih Fatimah screen after a prayer is now one smooth fade, with the prayer list open or closed.
  Try: Mark a prayer, tap "Post-salah tasbih?", count, then Finish and Done.

REMINDERS
- Your reminders: zikr reminders now sit in each day's list with the prayers, the days grid shows whole weeks, and Settings > Notifications always opens the page.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
