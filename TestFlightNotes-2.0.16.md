# TestFlight 2.0 (16): internal only (Newest builds)

From release/2.0 (d11ff28): 2.0 (15) plus watch fixes only. The phone app is the same as 2.0 (15).

## What to Test (paste this)

Apple Watch, zikr counter:
- Pinch (Double Tap) now counts right away, even after the screen has dimmed. It used to dim first and count about 2 seconds later.
- Counting with your fingers and with the Crown are now two separate ways. Tap or pinch to count, or turn the Crown on its own. In Crown mode, tapping the screen no longer counts or buzzes, and Double Tap is paused; the badge switches back.
- Taps above and below the ring now count too (before, only the middle did).
- Leaving the watch app now pauses the session, and your count is kept. Lowering your wrist does not pause it. If it pauses when you only lowered your wrist, please tell us.
- At each 100, and at each Tasbih Fatimah step, you feel one stronger tap instead of two.
