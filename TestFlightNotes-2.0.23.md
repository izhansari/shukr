What's new in this build. Thank you for testing!

GETTING STARTED
- A new setup: it opens on a hadith, then location, prayer times (method and Asr on one page), notifications with a live preview of the real banners, a real Fajr alarm, and the masjid nearest you. It ends on the welcome ring becoming your Salah circle.
- Zikr opens with its own short tour: why zikr matters, then you pick your first zikr and its daily task, practise the three ways to count, and count it for real.

SALAH
- A private photo for each prayer: after you mark it, take one with the front and back cameras at once. Photos stay on your phone.
- Memories (from the menu): your prayer photos by month, day or prayer, with search, favorites and "On this day". Tap a day to open its pile; swipe through the day's prayers, add a note in place, share a framed card.
- Prayers still to come are dashed on the list; in a prayer's last hour the time left stands out.
- Pull down on the Salah page to open the menu.

ZIKR
- The pause screen is calmer: the full zikr card at the top, your streak by the title.
- Share a task: hold a task and choose Share task. The task and its whole zikr (words, notes, voice memo, photo) go as one file that opens in shukr.
- Make a zikr's voice memo from a video.
- A new built-in zikr: the shahada.

APPLE WATCH
- Count with tap and pinch, or the Crown (the first turn picks the direction).
- Light, dark or auto look; the full prayer list, always; tap a time to flip it.
- Tap the Qibla arrow for a map that turns you toward Mecca.
- The zikr wheel glides on hard flicks; the pause screen has sets and Keeps going.

WIDGETS AND REMINDERS
- The Prayers widget has the soft look and shows time left in the last hour.
- The Fajr alarm on iOS 26.1 and later is a real system alarm set by shukr each day.

SMOOTHER
- Swiping between pages drops far fewer frames, and the app uses less battery while open.
