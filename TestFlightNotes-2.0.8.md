## What to Test (paste this)

A big update since the last build. Thank you for testing!

PRAYER
• New prayer scoring: Early (first 30 min) · On time · Late · Qaza. Your day score is the average of the five.
• Marking a prayer now has a moment: the ring closes in its score colour, with a haptic. Done prayers fold away in the list.
• The next prayer shows "NEXT" with a dashed ring, so you can tell it hasn't started yet.
• Streaks and "in-time days" (no Qaza), with small celebrations.
• Edit the time you prayed with a new time wheel.
• The prayer day now runs Fajr to Fajr, so a late Isha after midnight still counts for its day.
• "I already prayed" and "nudge me in 5 / 10 min" work right from the notification.

QIBLA & MAP
• The map turns so the line to the Kaaba points straight up your screen. Hold your phone out and turn until the streets match. It's worked out from your location, so it's right even when your compass isn't.
• A short guide explains each map mode (tap ? any time).
• Explore (the magnifying glass): your prayer spots (every prayer pinned where you prayed it, coloured by score) and a Mosque finder: nearest mosques with travel time, directions, Look Around and a list. Long-press a mosque to stop it being recommended.

ZIKR
• The Zikr page is a wheel of circles: freestyle plus your daily tasks, each with today's progress and a time estimate.
• New pause and results screens, with your mantra's full text and notes.
• Count in sets: switch on "+3" and every tap counts 3 (you'll feel a triple tick).
• Post-salah tasbih (Tasbih Fatimah, 33·33·34) in one flowing session.
• Mantras library and Zikr History with a 14-day chart.

NEW
• 99 Names of Allah, with flashcards.
• Daily Ayah redesigned: tap anywhere to reveal, with a share card for Stories.
• Insights: am I getting better, how am I scoring, how consistent am I.
• Home Screen widgets: Prayers, Zikr (tap a task to jump to it), Name of the Day, Daily Ayah.
• Lock Screen widgets, plus Qibla and Tasbeeh buttons for Control Center or the Lock Screen.
• A short welcome when you open the app.

PLEASE TRY
• Mark a prayer from the widget and from the notification.
• Use the qibla map outside and see if lining up with the streets makes sense.
• Add the Lock Screen widgets and a Control Center button.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
