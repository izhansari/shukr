## What to Test (paste this)

A big one: a new look and a reworked zikr counter. Thank you for testing!

NEW LOOK
- The Salah page has a new soft look: the prayers sit in a sunken card, grey-blue in light mode and charcoal in dark.
- As you mark prayers, the list makes room and rows fade in and out.

SALAH
- Mark a prayer by tapping its dot in the list, or by holding the circle. The ring sweeps closed and shows your grade and score.
- A prayer that hasn't started has a dashed ring. In the last hour of a prayer you haven't marked, the circle counts down the minutes left.
- After marking, a "Post-salah tasbih?" pill shows for 15 seconds.
- Jumu'ah at a masjid scores full.

ZIKR
- Tap a task's circle on the Zikr page: it opens into the counter.
- The pause screen: Resume and Finish at the top (Finish asks for a second tap), your pace in the big tile, time left and your finish time as one line, and round switches for continuous, sleep, count in sets and haptics. Hold a switch to learn more.
- Count in sets: pick a size once (each tap counts 5, say). While it's on, +5 shows at the top of the counter; tap it to turn sets off.
- Sleep mode dims the counter. If you fall asleep, the session is saved at your last tap and a good-morning card waits for you.
- Task streaks, and History rows you can open, swipe to delete, or hold for more.

SETUP AND SETTINGS
- A first-run setup: location, calculation method, madhab, light or dark, reminders, Fajr alarm and your masjid.
- Settings is tidier, and Refresh Location shows when it's done.

REMINDERS
- Prayer reminders are planned a week ahead, with a nudge halfway through and one with 30 minutes left for prayers you haven't marked.

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
- Prayers widget: the ring, a check to mark the prayer, and a tap for today's times. Lock Screen: the minutes left in a prayer's last hour.
- Apple Watch: today's prayers, marking, the qibla, zikr with the Digital Crown or Double Tap (watch Settings, Double Tap beta), Tasbih Fatimah and complications.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
