## What to Test (paste this)

A steadier qibla arrow, clearer prayer reminders, a reorganised Zikr tab and task streaks. Thank you for testing!

SALAH
- The qibla arrow on the circle uses the same steady heading as the Compass app (true north), keeps turning when you come back to the app or move around, and never says you're lined up before it knows where you are.
- If your iPhone's compass needs calibrating (near a magnetic case or car mount), the arrow turns dashed and "Compass needs calibration" appears under the circle. Tap it for a short guide.
- Prayer reminders now read like this: "Asr" with a coloured dot, "Pray by 6:48 PM" first, then a line that changes: at the start, 30 minutes in (when the ring turns yellow) and with 30 minutes left.

ZIKR
- The Zikr tab is reorganised: the wheel is today's tasks; History (clock, top left) and Azkar (books, top right) are their own pages; "Your tasks" opens from "N of M tasks done".
- New task: a short step-by-step flow. Hold a task anywhere for its options.
- Task streaks: meet a task's goal each day to build a streak, shown with a flame after a session, in Your tasks and in the Zikr title (tap it for your best).
- Sleep mode on the pause screen: if you fall asleep counting, the session is saved, ending at your last tap.
- A zikr's card has tabs for the full zikr, notes, a voice memo and a photo. Voice memos sound cleaner.
- Tap a session in History to open its own page.

MAP AND MOSQUES
- The map's sheet has no close button: switch layers with Explore (Qibla, Prayers, Mosques), which opens and closes more calmly.
- Duas at my masajid: the entering dua now comes as you arrive (a wider area round the masjid), including your first visit after starring it.

WIDGETS
- Lock Screen circle: in a prayer's last hour it shows the time left ("27m left") in place of the name, updating each minute.
- The Prayers widget plans the next prayer ahead, so its colours and countdown switch on time.

REMINDERS
- Fajr alarm (iOS 26.1 and later): shukr sets real system alarms itself. Settings has a Test alarm to try it.

APPLE WATCH
- Freestyle on the watch: pick one of your azkar from the chip inside the Freestyle circle, or just count.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
