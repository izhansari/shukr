Privacy policy · effective TODO date

The short version: shukr has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. What you do in shukr stays on your device.

What shukr keeps, and where

Your prayer records, scores and streaks, your zikr sessions and tasks, your azkar with their notes, voice memos and photos, and your settings are stored on your iPhone (and, for what the Watch app needs, on your Apple Watch). They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is Apple's and yours, not ours. We cannot see any of it.

Location

With your permission, shukr uses your location on your device to work out prayer times and the qibla, to pin each prayer where you prayed it, to find mosques near you and, if you turn it on, to show a dua when you arrive at your masjid. With "Always" permission, prayer times and reminders follow you when you travel, even when the app is closed. You can instead pick a city and give no location at all.

Your location is never sent to us. For the city name, the map, mosque search and directions, shukr uses Apple's own location and maps services on your device (Core Location and MapKit), which are covered by Apple's privacy policy. Opening directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze hands the destination to that app.

Microphone and camera

Only when you record a voice memo or take a photo for one of your azkar. The recording and the photo are stored with your azkar on your device and nowhere else.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer reminders, zikr reminders and the Fajr alarm are scheduled on your device by the app. No push server is involved.

Apple Watch

The Watch app and the iPhone app exchange prayer marks, zikr sessions and settings directly between your devices through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Nothing passes through us.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is asked for.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The only third-party code is an open-source prayer-time calculation library (adhan-swift), which runs entirely on your device.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data is sold or shared.

Beta builds

If you use a TestFlight build and choose to send feedback from the app, you share it yourself through the iOS share sheet; nothing is sent unless you do that. Apple's TestFlight collects crash logs and the feedback you send through TestFlight under Apple's terms.

Children

shukr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it kept goes with it (and from your Watch when its app is removed). Backups follow your device's own backup settings.
