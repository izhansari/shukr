What's new in this build. Thank you for testing!

GETTING STARTED
- A short tour after setup: Prayer Circle, Prayer List, Zikr and Settings. A quick look at each, then you try it on a practice prayer. Run it again any time from the menu or Settings.
- Zikr Tour: make your first task (Astaghfirullah, 33 a day), count it for real, then see your streak and your pace. It's offered when you first open the Zikr page, and stays in the menu until you finish it.
- Your first freestyle session shows a few counting tips.

ZIKR
- The Zikr page: freestyle first, then your tasks still to do today, then New task. Freestyle has a "Pick a zikr" button to save the session under a zikr.
- The line under the wheel opens Your tasks: hold and drag to reorder, tap to edit, swipe to delete.
- Each session's haptics can be turned down or off from the pause screen.

SALAH
- A prayer that hasn't started yet has a dashed ring; it fills in when the prayer begins.
- In a prayer's last hour, the circle shows the time left.
- After your last prayer, the ring shows your day: each prayer in its color, a dot where you prayed it, and one line for tomorrow.
- The bottom bar (Zikr, Salah, Settings) and the pages always agree.

WIDGETS
- Tapping a widget goes straight to what it shows.
- Prayers widget: the ring with a mark-as-prayed check, your choice of corners and style, and today's times, where you can mark a prayer.

APPLE WATCH
- Today's prayers with the phone's ring, marking and unmarking, the qibla, zikr with the phone's counter (tap, Double Tap or the Crown), Tasbih Fatimah, and complications.

FIXES
- Opening Your tasks no longer makes the phone run hot.
