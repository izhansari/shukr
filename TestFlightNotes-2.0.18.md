## What to Test (paste this)

New for the Apple Watch, and a reworked pause screen. Thank you for testing!

APPLE WATCH
- Double Tap: the watch's Settings has Double Tap (beta) with three ways to count: Scroll (the default), Scroll auto, and a count button. Try a session with each and say which feels best.
- The watch works out prayer times from the same method table as the phone, so its times match.
- Syncing is steadier: marks made on the watch reach the phone reliably, and a new or switched watch gets the phone's data.

ZIKR
- Pause screen: the pause button turns into Resume and the minus into Finish (Finish asks for a second tap). The zikr's name, memo and photo sit inside its card; tap the card to edit the zikr. Round switches below, count in sets included, and the time left as one quiet line at the bottom.
- The rate tile: tap it to switch between per count and per tasbeeh.
- Results: Done at the bottom. If you fell asleep counting, the button is Keep counting.

SALAH
- Pull down on the Salah page: the arrow by your city spins while your location updates, then a tick.
- A snoozed prayer reminder no longer arrives after you mark the prayer.
- Each prayer now keeps its prayer day (Fajr to Fajr). This is a one-time update on first launch; your data is backed up first.

SETTINGS
- Location is one row: tap it to update.
- Fewer extra rows.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
