## What to Test (paste this)

What's new since the last build. Thank you for testing!

APPLE WATCH
- Pinching to count: every pinch counts, even fast ones.
  Try: Start a zikr on the watch and pinch quickly. Each pinch adds one.
- Tasbih Fatimah: the ring fills once per phrase (33, 33, 34), on the phone and the watch.
  Try: After a prayer, start the post-salah tasbih and watch the ring fill and start again for each phrase.

REMINDERS
- Your reminders has a new, calmer page (Settings > Notifications > tap the status row).
  Try: Tap a day: each prayer lists every reminder it gets (start, halfway, 30 min left) and the day's total.
  Try: Each prayer: switch a prayer off, start or nudge and see how far ahead reminders are scheduled.
  Try: Notification settings at the bottom: a green shield means your settings let reminders through on time.
- Fewer reminders a day now schedule further ahead (up to 13 days).

ZIKR
- Editing a task opens a review page: tap Zikr, Goal or Place to change it, then Save.
  Try: Your tasks > tap a task.

SALAH
- Pulling down on the Salah page says "Keep pulling to refresh", then "Let go to refresh location".

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
