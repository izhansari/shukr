# shukr 2.0 (11) — What to Test

Built from 80b45fb + the build bump. Released with `scripts/asc.py release 11`.

## What to Test (paste this)

Smaller fixes on top of build 10. Thank you for testing!

APPLE WATCH
- The watch's prayer ring now matches the phone: it fills as the prayer's time passes and changes colour with the score you'd get (green, then yellow, then red). Before a prayer starts it shows NEXT with a dashed ring. Tap the ring to switch between the end time and the time left.
- The watch complications fill the same way, in the same colours.

ZIKR
- Azkar: the filter button is now a Sort and Filter menu. Sort by most or least recited, recently used, slowest or fastest pace, or A-Z, and turn the built-in azkar on or off.

WHAT'S NEW (tap the build line at the bottom of the menu or Settings)
- Notes that a change has addressed no longer count as unsent.
- "Still off" always starts a fresh note and never copies or overwrites a note you haven't sent yet.

MAP
- The qibla buzz keeps a steady once-a-second rhythm even when the compass wobbles, and stays quiet while the map guide is open.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
