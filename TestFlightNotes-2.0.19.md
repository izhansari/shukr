## What to Test (paste this)

What's new since the last build. Thank you for testing!

APPLE WATCH
- Pinching to count is fixed: every pinch counts, even fast ones.
  Try: Start a zikr session on the watch and pinch as fast as you can. The number goes up with every pinch.
  Try: Tap the screen once, then pinch again. It still counts.
  Try: Turn the Digital Crown. Each click counts one.

SALAH
- Pulling down on the Salah page (list closed) now says "Keep pulling to refresh", then "Let go to refresh", and refreshes your location.
  Try: Pull down on the Salah page and watch the small line at the bottom.

REMINDERS
- Settings > Notifications has a "Your reminders" row again; it opens the page showing every reminder waiting on your phone.
  Try: Settings > Notifications > Your reminders.

SETTINGS
- The Sneak peek sheets all open at the same size.
  Try: Settings > Sneak peek: open Food Finder, then the others.

Found something off? Send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.
